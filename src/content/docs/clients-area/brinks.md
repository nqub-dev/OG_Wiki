## Overview

nqub delivers end-to-end ATM expertise across the full device lifecycle to
secure, build, deploy, and respond. Several of our capabilities have no
equivalent inside or outside the institutions we serve.

## ATM Product & Security Services

### Security Consulting

- **Security standards & frameworks** — authorship of ATM security
  configuration requirements, control-tier definitions, and the contractual
  baseline for cash-liability coverage
- **Software security** — OS hardening, BIOS/firmware, network, and golden
  image development
- **Penetration testing & vulnerability research** — DMA/PCIe attack
  testing, threat-surface analysis, and chipset-level security assessment
- **Attack response & incident forensics** — rapid response and root-cause
  analysis for hook-and-chain, jackpotting, black-box, and ram-raid events,
  with cash-loss reduction as the measured outcome
- **Physical security** — controls addressing hook-and-chain, ram raid,
  brute-force safe attack, and unauthorized physical access

### Hardware & Software Development

- **Hardware & manufacturing** — chipset selection, board security, custom
  manufacturing specifications, and chassis design
- **Industrial design & digital twin services**
- **Regression testing** — structured test suites validating fleet-wide
  changes prior to deployment

### UI/UX Design & Screenflow Development

- **UI/UX & screen development** — interface design (Figma) and
  screen-flow development for ATM software platforms

### Platform & Fleet Operations

- **Fleet compliance & managed monitoring** — device-level telemetry,
  RMM-based fleet visibility, and continuous compliance mapping against
  security requirements
- **Platform: [[nqub-products/geopulse|GeoPulse]]** — nqub's proprietary
  platform for ATM asset management, fleet intelligence, and attack response

### Advisory & Solution Scoping

- **Advisory & solution scoping** — requirements facilitation, feasibility
  review, and client workshops
