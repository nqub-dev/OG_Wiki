## What this is

The nqub team wiki — one place for onboarding info, our brand assets, what
each of our products does, and what we're doing for each client.

## Where to go

- **[[handbook/onboarding]]** — new here? Start with this.
- **[[handbook/brand]]** — logos, colors, and how to use them.
- **nqub Products** (sidebar) — one page per product.
- **Clients Area** (sidebar) — what we're doing, and for whom.

## Finding things

Press **⌘K** (or `/`) to search. Every page also lists what links to it, at
the bottom — a wiki earns its keep when pages reference each other, so link
generously with `[[section/page]]`.

## Editing

Click **"Edit this page"** at the top of any page. If you don't have edit
access yet, ask an admin to grant you the `Wiki Edit` permission.
